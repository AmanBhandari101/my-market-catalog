# My Market — product catalogue photos

Small product photos (320 px, WebP) used by the My Market shop app's product search.
Served through jsDelivr at `https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/AmanBhandari101/my-market-catalog@main/p/<id>.webp`,
where `<id>` is `bc-<barcode>` for packaged products.

## Sources and licences
- **Packaged products:** photos come from Open Food Facts, Open Beauty Facts and Open Products Facts
  (contributors of those projects), licensed **CC BY-SA 3.0**. They are cleaned up (background
  removed, centred on white) and stay under **CC BY-SA 3.0**: you may reuse them with the same
  credit and licence.
- Pack photos show brand names and logos; those trademarks belong to their owners. The photos are
  here only to identify the product in a shop's stock and bills.
- Each product's source page is `https://world.openfoodfacts.org/product/<barcode>`
  (or the Beauty / Products Facts equivalent), where the original photo and its history live.

To ask for a photo to be removed, open an issue in this repository.
